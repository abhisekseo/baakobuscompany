What customers say

Questions

Is my request a confirmed booking?

No. BAAKO reviews each request and confirms it directly with you before a trip is booked.

Who can request transportation?

Schools, universities, churches, youth groups, sports teams, day care centers, park districts, civic organizations, corporations and private groups.

What safety practices does BAAKO follow?

Rigorous hiring, background checks, daily bus inspections, a child check policy, GPS technology and preventative maintenance. See the safety section above.

Do you offer child safety seats?

Child safety restraint systems are available for ages 3–5. Mention this in your request.

How else can I reach BAAKO?
